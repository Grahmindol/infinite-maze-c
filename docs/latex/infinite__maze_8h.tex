Retrieve hierarchical dead-\/end information for a world cell. 

Encodes walkability and hierarchical fixedness of the cell located at world coordinates ({\ttfamily wx}, {\ttfamily wy}).

A cell is considered {\itshape fixed} if it lies on the path connecting regions at the corresponding hierarchy level. Consequently, the more bits that are set, the more globally important the cell is:\+ highly fixed cells are shared by increasingly large-\/scale paths through the maze.

A bit set to {\ttfamily 1} indicates that the corresponding cell (or its ancestor at that hierarchy level) is fixed.
